My Voice

This the timeline for regaining my voice.

  • November 7th, 2023 Laryngectomy

  • December 4th, 2023 Meeting with SLP

    • This is the meeting where, when using the Electrolarynx, the SLP pretended to understand what I was saying.
    • That is, she was lip-reading as I was voicing (Deirdre suggested that this was what she was doing (during the April 1st meeting, she acknowledged that she could lip read)). Even though I was completely unintelligible (to both myself and Deirdre when listening to the audio) she pretended to understand what I was saying (Part of me feels that she is a shill for ATOS and is trying to get me to use my insurance to buy stuff that I don't need)

  • December 11th, 2023 First Voicing Session

    • This was exciting. I made my first "noise" in over a month. I wasn't sure how difficult it would be and was surprised how easy it was. I wasn't to know that it would be downhill for the next 4 months
    • Here I am making noise:

  • December 13th, 2023 Swelling

    • A few days after I began speaking, there was severe swelling on my neck. I was later to find out that this was called lymphedema and is common. Because of this, I took a few days off from speaking (to ask about the swelling during my next meeting with the SLP)

  • December 18th, 2023 Replacement TEP

    • By the time of my next SLP appointment, my TEP developed a leak.
    • Little was I to know that this would be the start of a process that was as unnecessary as it was frustrating.
    • The TEP didn't work from day one.

  • December 27th, 2023 No Voice

    • November 7th, 2023 Laryngectomy

  • January 2nd, 2024 No Voice

    • November 7th, 2023 Laryngectomy

  • January 10th, 2024 Replace Current Prosthesis: 8mm/17f provox vega with New Prosthesis: 16f,8mm In health classic

    • " Pt able to achieve voicing with and without fenestrated lary tube with both prosthesis, but endorses ease of communication with new In Health TEP."

  • January 17th, 2024 Watched YouTube video for lymphedema?

    • Bizarre waste of time.

  • February 1st, 2024 Laryngectomy

  • February 9th, 2024 Laryngectomy

  • February 21st, 2024 Laryngectomy

Even though I was not communicating clearly, there was no further treatment for the next 6 weeks.
The unsuccessful voicing was blamed on either lymphedema or on my narrow esophagus.
As we find out, over 2 months later, the amount of air flowing through the TEP is (yet again) another important feature

SPICEY BAKED HAM



INGREDIENTS

  • 2kg/4lbs joint ham (approx)
  • 50g/2oz brown sugar
  • 50g/2oz bread crumbs
  • 2 tsp mustard
  • 1 level tsp Goodalls Ground Ginger
  • 125ml\1⁄4 pint of cider or pineapple juice approx
  • Goodalls Whole Cloves to decorate


METHOD

  1. Steep the ham in cold water overnight.
  2. Next day put in fresh cold water in a deep saucepan.
  3. Bring to the boil and remove any scum. Cover and simmer very gently allowing 20 minutes per lb and 20 minutes over.
  4. When cooked, remove from water, remove the skin, but not the fat.
  5. Spread the mustard over the fat and press the brown sugar in a layer on top. Cut the fat in criss-cross fashion, and stick a clove in the corner of each diamond shape. Put on a tin.
  6. Bake in the oven at 200°C, 400°F, Gas Mark 6 for about 30-40 mins. Baste (spoon) some of the cider or pineapple juice over the ham from time to time during cooking.
  7. Cook until nice and crispy. Remove meat from oven and allow it to relax for 15 minutes before carving.
  8. Note: A little more cider and some blended cornflour can be added to the juices in the dish, to make a delicious cider sauce.

Christmas Pudding



INGREDIENTS

  • 2 pint bowl (plastic, with lid)
  • 4 oz softened luxury margarine or butter.
  • 4 oz soft brown sugar
  • 2 eggs standard
  • Grated rind and juice of 1 orange
  • 2 tablespoons of brandy
  • 4 tablespoons of Guinness
  • A small apple peeled, cored and grated
  • 4 oz fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 2 oz cherries, quartered
  • 2 oz of blanched chopped almonds
  • 2 oz mixed peel
  • 6 oz currants
  • 6 oz sultanas
  • 4 oz raisins
  • 4 oz plain flour
  • Quarter-teaspoon (level) of salt sieved together with a quarter-teaspoon of nutmeg and a quarter-teaspoon of mixed spices.


Method

  1. Melt the margarine and sugar together slowly at low heat in a saucepan.
  2. Put the cherries, almonds, peel, currants, sultanas and raisins in a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the grated apple and the orange-rind and juice, mixing gently as you go. Add the breadcrumbs, and sieve in the flour. Add the mixed salt and spices. Mix.
  4. Add melted margarine and sugar, Guinness, and brandy.
  5. Beat the eggs separately, and add to the mix. Mix all together.
  6. Grease the plastic lidded bowl and fill it with the mixed ingredients. Cut two large rounds of greaseproof paper and slit the sides so they cover the pudding closely.
  7. Put a thin layer of flour over the greaseproof paper - this forms a seal when steamed.
  8. Burthe lid on the bowl. Cover with two large rounds of greaseproof paper which lap down the outside of the bowl. Tie firmly in place with string, including a loop for lifting if desired.
  9. In a pot big enough to contain the bowl under its lid, bring sufficient water to the boil to come about half-way up the bowl. A plate in the bottom of the pot will protect the plastic bowl from direct heat.
  10. Place the bowl in the pot and simmer for 6 hours, checking and topping up the boiling water as required.
  11. When cooked, allow to cool before turning the pudding out onto a plate. Wrap in greaseproof paper and return to the (washed) bowl until Christmas
  12. Good luck!

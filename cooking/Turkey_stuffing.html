TURKEY STUFFING



Ingredients

  • 2 Ib pork sausage meat
  • 1 heaped desert spoon dried sage (or mixed herbs)
  • 1 large onion, grated or chopped very finely
  • 4 heaped tablespoons white breadcrumbs
  • 2-3 tablespoons boiling water (or soft margarine)
  • Salt
  • Freshly milled black pepper


Procedure

  1. Combine breadcrumbs with onion and sage in large mixing bowl.
  2. Stir in a little boiling water and mix thoroughly.
  3. Work the sausage meat into the mixture.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. If you like it crumbly, leave it like this
  6. If you want to slice it, add a beaten egg to bind the mixture.
  7. Parcel in greaseproof paper and wrap in tinfoil. This is safer than cooking it inside the turkey. Roast with the turkey.
